Luna Camp — free brains only (Grok off by default)
====================================================

Can Grok-in-chat give you a free API key?
-----------------------------------------
No. The assistant cannot attach its own xAI/Grok key to your PC or Render.
That would still be “your account” billing or simply isn’t shareable.
Use free compute instead (below).

Most efficient free stack
-------------------------
1) Ollama on YOUR PC (best) — electricity only, no API tokens
     ollama serve
     ollama pull llama3.2
2) Groq free tier — fast cloud Llama, free quota (you sign up)
     https://console.groq.com → API key → GROQ_API_KEY in .env
3) OpenRouter free models — models ending in :free
     https://openrouter.ai → key → OPENROUTER_API_KEY
     OPENROUTER_MODEL=meta-llama/llama-3.1-8b-instruct:free
4) Gemini free (optional) — GEMINI_API_KEY
5) Aether templates — last resort monologues (no LLM bill)

Priority chain (per agent)
--------------------------
1) Ollama (if up)
2) Groq free
3) OpenRouter :free
4) Gemini free
5) Aether

Grok (xAI) is OFF unless you deliberately opt in (costs YOUR credits):
  LUNA_ALLOW_GROK=1
  LUNA_DISABLE_GROK=0
  LUNA_GROK_FALLBACK=1
  XAI_API_KEY=...

@a Ara / @m Mika use the same free chain (not Grok-only).

Efficiency knobs (.env)
-----------------------
  FREE_MAX_TOKENS=360

  GROQ_MODEL=llama-3.1-8b-instant

--------------

LUNA_LLM_BACKEND=ollama
OLLAMA_HOST=http://127.0.0.1:11434
OLLAMA_MODEL=llama3.2

Env (Render / telephanti.com)
-----------------------------
Ollama is NOT on Render free. Live free talk needs ONE free cloud key:

  Recommended: GROQ_API_KEY from https://console.groq.com (free tier, Llama models)
  Or: OPENROUTER_API_KEY + free model meta-llama/llama-3.1-8b-instruct:free

Paste the key in Render → luna service → Environment → Save (redeploy).

Also force free (already in render.yaml):
  LUNA_FORCE_OLLAMA=0
  LUNA_ALLOW_GROK=0
  LUNA_DISABLE_GROK=1
  LUNA_GROK_FALLBACK=0
  LUNA_FREE_BRAINS=1
  LUNA_LLM_BACKEND=free

Without any free key, live uses aether offline templates (no bill, weaker replies).

Check: https://telephanti.com/api/health
  free_minds=true and free_cloud.groq=true  → real free LLM live

Luna Camp — free brains only (Grok off by default)
====================================================

Priority chain (per agent)
--------------------------
1) Ollama (local, free) — primary on your PC
     hermes3 / llama3.2
2) Groq free tier       — GROQ_API_KEY
3) Google Gemini free   — GEMINI_API_KEY or GOOGLE_API_KEY
4) OpenRouter free      — OPENROUTER_API_KEY
5) Aether templates     — offline last resort (no paid bill)

Grok (xAI) is OFF unless you deliberately opt in:
  LUNA_ALLOW_GROK=1
  LUNA_DISABLE_GROK=0
  LUNA_GROK_FALLBACK=1
  XAI_API_KEY=...

@a Ara / @m Mika use the same free chain (not Grok-only).
